Network Traffic Monitor & Firewall (GlassWire Alternative)
Concept: tpt-netguard – A visual, easy-to-understand network activity monitor.
Tech Stack: Rust (for low-level packet inspection and Windows Filtering Platform integration) + TypeScript (for beautiful real-time graphs).
Why it’s better: GlassWire has become heavily monetized and restrictive. An open-source alternative can provide real-time per-app bandwidth usage, historical data, and one-click firewall blocking rules without hiding basic features behind a $40/year paywall.

Compatible on Windows, Linux and Archon. https://github.com/tpt-solutions/tpt-archon

Linux & Archon Compatibility: Uses nftables/eBPF on Linux. On Archon, it hooks directly into the microkernel's user-space networking stack, providing real-time, capability-aware traffic monitoring and blocking without the performance penalty of traditional kernel-mode context switches.
